# Your Acaciana Fub playtest

From Ross to you

I've made a small playable test from your Acaciana Fub ideas. You can move around, collect a sword, fight an enemy, open a passage and recover a memory.

The gold circle stands in for Acaciana. The other shapes, colours and rooms are temporary. I have left the memory's story text blank because I want your version of what happens. When you see CANON_TBD, it means that story detail still needs to be agreed.

I'd like you to try it and tell me what you expected, what confused you and what you want me to keep. You can disagree with anything I have put in. You can also draw your answer or leave a question for later.

First, see what you can work out from the screen. I can help if you get stuck. This is a test of the game, not a test of you.

Ross

## Controls when you need them

- Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move; E to use; J or Space to attack; R to restart. E or Escape closes the memory. M turns sound off/on.
- Touch: drag the round movement pad; tap USE near an object; hold ATTACK once you have the sword. Continue closes the memory. Restart starts again. The Sound button turns sound off/on. Landscape gives more room.
- Standard controller: stick or D-pad to move; bottom button (A) to use; left button (X) to attack; right button (B) to close the memory; Menu/Start to restart; top button (Y) for sound. A also closes the memory. Labels use Xbox-style names.

Your health can reach zero. Restart restores the whole test. You can stop whenever you want.
